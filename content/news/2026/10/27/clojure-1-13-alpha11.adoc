== Try it out

Update your deps.edn :deps with:

[source]
----
org.clojure/clojure {:mvn/version "1.13.0-alpha11"}
----

Start a REPL with the Clojure CLI (any version) with:

[source]
----
clj -Sdeps '{:deps {org.clojure/clojure {:mvn/version "1.13.0-alpha11"}}}'
----
