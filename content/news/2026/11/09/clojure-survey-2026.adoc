It's time for the annual State of Clojure Survey!

If you use Clojure, ClojureScript, or any other Clojure dialect, we would love to get your responses to the following survey:

* https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/clojure2026[State of Clojure 2026]

This survey has been running since 2010 and serves as the most useful barometer we have of what the Clojure community is thinking about and using day to day. It just takes a few minutes (most questions are optional, feel free to skip) but really is an important contribution.

The survey will close Nov 30 so that we can release the results towards the end of the year. Thanks!
